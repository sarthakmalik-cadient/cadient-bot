10. Data Retention Policy
10.1 Overview
Cadient is committed to protecting candidate and client data while balancing compliance, security, and operational needs. Our standard data retention policy ensures that applicant information is retained for a defined period and securely purged thereafter, unless longer retention is required by law or agreed with the client.
10.2 Standard Retention Periods
•   Standard Retention: Candidate application data is retained in production systems for three (3) years. After this period, applications are automatically purged, with exceptions for statuses such as Hired and Terminated.
•   California-Specific: For clients located in California, applicant data is retained for four (4) years to comply with state law.
•   Data Warehouse: Data in the reporting warehouse is managed separately and is not subject to the standard production data retention cycle.
10.3 Client-Defined Retention Rules
Through Cadient DMS (Data Management Service), clients can define their own retention rules:
•   Specifying a different age for application data (e.g., two years instead of three)
•   Designating status-based exceptions for deletion (e.g., retaining Hired, Offered, or Terminated applications)
10.4 Compliance and Legal Holds
•   Regulatory Alignment: Cadient's practices align with CCPA, GDPR (where applicable), and state-specific employment recordkeeping requirements.
•   Legal Holds: When client data is subject to a litigation hold or regulatory investigation, no applications will be purged until the hold is released.
10.5 Secure Deletion & Irretrievability
When candidate data reaches the end of its retention period, the data is securely purged using industry-standard methods. Once deleted, data cannot be restored. Clients should carefully review retention settings in Cadient DMS.

OVERVIEW
Cadient SmartHire integrates with a comprehensive ecosystem of best-in-class partners across the entire employment lifecycle. Rather than building every capability in-house or locking customers into single-vendor solutions, Cadient has established partnerships with leading providers in background screening, payroll, compliance, job distribution, assessment, and other critical hiring functions.

Cadient Decision Point
AI-Powered Prediction of Job Tenure
Overview
Cadient Decision Point is an augmented intelligence solution designed to predict job tenure—the expected length of time a candidate is likely to remain employed with an organization.
Decision Point helps employers identify candidates who are more likely to stay long-term, supporting reduced turnover and improved workforce stability. It is important to position the solution correctly:
It predicts likelihood of long-term employment
It anticipates expected time period to stay on the job
It identifies candidates with a higher probability of extended tenure
Decision Point does not predict job performance. While longer-tenured employees may also perform well, the solution is specifically engineered to forecast tenure outcomes—not productivity, sales results, or cultural fit.
How Decision Point Works
Data Inputs
Decision Point analyzes structured data collected through the employment application, such as:
Work history
Employment gaps
Referral sources
Job-related experience
Application responses
The model does not rely on self-reported personality or attitude measures (those belong to assessments). Instead, it uses existing application data and correlates it with historical tenure outcomes.
Algorithm Development
Depending on the selected offering, the algorithm is either:
Built using industry-level tenure patterns, or
Custom-developed using the client’s historical employee data
For custom models, Cadient’s Data Science team:
Analyzes employees with the longest tenure
Identifies predictive factors
Develops a tenure-based machine learning model unique to the organization
Ongoing or batch employee data is used to validate that the model continues performing as intended.
Results & User Experience
Decision Point results are displayed inside the hiring workflow as five easy-to-interpret score bands. Raw predictor scores are not visible.

Decision Point results are intended to be one input among several, alongside interviews, references, and assessments. Cadient does not prescribe specific hiring thresholds; usage should align with each organization’s internal hiring strategy.
Bias Monitoring & Compliance
Decision Point includes responsible AI safeguards:
Pre- and post-implementation adverse impact monitoring
Review of EEO data (race/ethnicity and gender)
Ongoing validation to prevent unintended bias
Documentation of predictor variables and impact analysis
Cadient reserves the right to refine model weights, exclude predictors, or adjust the algorithm to improve business utility and ensure legal compliance.
The goal is to increase average tenure in a fair and unbiased manner.

Data Pull Fee
Understanding how data extraction charges work within Cadient’s SmartSuite™

What Is the Data Pull Fee?
The Data Pull Fee is a transactional maintenance charge applied whenever data is extracted, exported, or retrieved in bulk from Cadient’s platform databases, reporting engine, or integration layer. As organizations run SmartSuite™ at scale—processing thousands of applicants and managing integrations with HRIS systems—data retrieval activity becomes a significant and billable operational event.
This fee exists because large-scale data extraction consumes backend compute, storage I/O, and network bandwidth. Cadient’s pricing model separates base platform access from data retrieval intensity, ensuring that clients who extract data infrequently are not subsidizing the infrastructure costs of high-volume data consumers.

Calculation Methodology
Cadient calculates Data Pull Fees using a tiered model that accounts for both frequency and volume. The baseline contract tier typically includes a defined number of pull events per month—commonly 10–20 for mid-market clients, up to 50 for enterprise. Pull events beyond this threshold are rated at the contracted overage rate.
Record-volume charges apply when a single pull event exceeds a defined record threshold (e.g., 5,000 records). Clients with complex multi-region HRIS integrations should work with their Cadient account team to model expected monthly extraction volumes and negotiate a right-sized allotment upfront.

Overage Fee
Understanding usage thresholds, what triggers overage charges, and how to keep costs under control

What Is the Overage Fee?
The Overage Fee is a variable surcharge that activates when an organization’s actual consumption of Cadient SmartSuite™ resources exceeds the usage thresholds defined in their service agreement. Every Cadient contract specifies baseline usage limits covering dimensions such as active requisitions, assessed candidates, texting credits, and API calls. When real-world usage outpaces these limits, overage charges are calculated and billed.
Overage Fees are the most unpredictable cost category in the Cadient maintenance structure because they are directly tied to operational activity. A seasonal hiring spike, a new market expansion, or an increase in candidate texting volume can each trigger charges that were not anticipated in the annual budget. Proactive monitoring and threshold management are the most effective defenses against unexpected overage bills.

What is E-Verify Integration?
The E-Verify Integration is a specialized post-hire solution that connects the Kronos Hiring Management Console (HMC) with USVerify to automate employment eligibility verification. This tool allows employers to electronically confirm that their new hires are authorized to work in the United States by cross-referencing Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and Social Security Administration (SSA) records.

By digitizing the Form I-9 process, this integration eliminates paper-based errors, ensures federal compliance, and provides a secure, centralized repository for all employment authorization documents.

Key Uses & Business Benefits
Seamless Electronic I-9s: Complete valid Form I-9s electronically during the onboarding process with data pre-populated from the applicant's profile to save time and reduce manual entry errors.
Automated Expiration Alerts: Receive proactive alerts before an employee’s work authorization documents expire, allowing them ample time to apply for renewals and maintaining continuous compliance.
Flexible Completion Methods: Supports multiple ways to complete the I-9, including in-person (Hiring Manager and employee together), remote via email link, or by uploading scanned paper copies.
Real-Time Status Tracking: View continuous status updates—from "Initiated" to "Employment Authorized"—directly within the HMC Hire Grid and Application Overview.
Audit-Ready Storage: Securely store and retrieve both active and inactive I-9 forms anytime for governmental inspections, ensuring your organization is always prepared for a DOL or DHS audit.
TNC Management: Simplifies the "Tentative Non-Confirmation" (TNC) process by allowing managers and hires to electronically sign notification and referral letters if initial verification fails.

How It Works: Example Usage
The integration is designed to be a natural extension of the standard hiring workflow.
1. Initiating the Verification
Once a new hire completes the initial onboarding, the "I-9 and E-Verify" workflow becomes available in the Hiring Process tab of the HMC. Clicking "Initiate" uses Single Sign-On (SSO) to securely open a USVerify window without requiring a separate login.

2. Pre-Populated Accuracy
When the "New I-9" method is selected, the system automatically pulls the employee's personal info into Section 1. For security, sensitive fields like Date of Birth and SSN are left blank for the employee to enter personally.
3. Seamless Dashboard Access
Managers can access the full USVerify Dashboard directly from the HMC home page via "Quick Links". This dashboard provides a snapshot of:

DHS cases requiring action.
Upcoming documentation expirations.
Statistical usage and compliance reporting by division.

HOW SMARTFEEDBACK WORKS
Voice-Based Conversational Surveys
SmartFeedback conducts check-ins through voice-based phone surveys using conversational AI. Rather than asking employees to complete online forms or respond to email questionnaires, the system initiates phone conversations where an AI agent asks questions and employees respond naturally by speaking. This voice-first approach works particularly well for shift workers and hourly employees who may not regularly check email or have convenient computer access.
The conversational format feels more personal and less formal than traditional surveys, encouraging candid responses. Employees can answer in their own words through free-text voice responses rather than being limited to rating scales or multiple choice options. The AI captures and transcribes these open-ended responses for analysis.
Strategic Timing and Customization
SmartFeedback automatically triggers check-ins at configured intervals after an employee's hire date. Common schedules include check-ins at day 1 (first day feedback), day 30 (first month reflection), and day 90 (approaching the end of the critical retention window). Organizations can fully customize both the timing of check-ins and the specific questions asked to align with their onboarding process and retention priorities.
While SmartFeedback is designed primarily for new hire engagement, the system can be expanded to include existing employees for ongoing pulse checks, culture surveys, or other feedback initiatives beyond the initial 90-day period.
No-Login, Frictionless Access
Employees don't need to log into any system or remember passwords to participate. SmartFeedback reaches out to employees via phone, making participation as simple as answering a call. This frictionless approach removes the barriers that typically suppress response rates for frontline workers, helping SmartFeedback achieve approximately 70% response rates - far higher than traditional email surveys.
Anonymous Feedback Collection
Employees can choose to provide feedback anonymously, encouraging honest responses about sensitive topics like management issues, workload concerns, or workplace problems they might not want attributed to them. The system allows employees to fully anonymize themselves before the conversation begins, ensuring they feel safe being candid about their experiences.
This anonymization is critical for capturing the unfiltered truth about what's working and what isn't during the crucial early days of employment. Anonymous feedback often reveals issues that would never surface in attributed surveys or manager conversations.
AI Sentiment Analysis
SmartFeedback uses AI to analyze employee responses and extract meaningful insights. The sentiment analysis detects both general positivity or negativity in responses and identifies specific themes such as management concerns, workload issues, training gaps, cultural misalignment, or satisfaction with specific aspects of the job.
Rather than requiring HR teams to manually read through hundreds of free-text responses and identify patterns, the AI automatically categorizes feedback into actionable themes and flags responses that indicate retention risk. This automation turns what would be an overwhelming amount of qualitative data into structured insights that can guide intervention and improvement efforts.
Dashboards and Real-Time Alerts
SmartFeedback surfaces insights through comprehensive reporting:

Dashboards: Visual displays of sentiment trends, theme prevalence, and response rates

Location-filtered reports: Managers can view feedback specific to their location or team

Individual insights: See feedback from specific at-risk employees (when not anonymous)

Aggregate trends: Understand patterns across the entire organization

Industry benchmarks: Compare your results against industry averages for context

Real-time alerts: Immediate notification when responses indicate serious concerns
These reporting capabilities ensure insights don't get buried in data - they're surfaced to the right people in actionable formats. While the system provides alerts for critical issues, it does not automatically recommend actions or enable direct responses to employees. Organizations establish their own processes for following up on feedback and addressing identified concerns.
INTEGRATION WITH SMARTSUITE
Feeding SmartTenure Predictions
SmartFeedback's most powerful integration is with SmartTenure, Cadient's AI-powered retention prediction tool. The feedback data collected from new hires - what they say about their early experiences, the sentiment they express, the themes that emerge - all feeds into SmartTenure's algorithms to improve future retention predictions.
This creates a closed learning loop: SmartTenure predicts which candidates are likely to stay long-term before they're hired, those predictions are tested against actual outcomes, and SmartFeedback captures the real reasons people stay or leave. This feedback continuously refines SmartTenure's models, making predictions progressively more accurate as the system learns what early indicators truly correlate with retention in your specific organization and roles.
SmartScore Relationship
SmartFeedback does not directly affect SmartScore, the composite candidate evaluation framework used during hiring. SmartScore is based on pre-hire assessment data (SmartMatch resume matching and SmartScreen phone screening), while SmartFeedback operates post-hire. The two serve different purposes in different phases of the employment lifecycle.
However, the insights SmartFeedback provides about which types of candidates succeed and remain engaged do indirectly influence the hiring intelligence loop by informing SmartTenure, which in turn affects hiring decisions. This creates a complete system where hiring, onboarding, and retention insights all reinforce each other.

SmartFeedback insights appear in dedicated reports rather than being embedded in the candidate tracking views of SmartHire or other recruiting tools. This separation makes sense since SmartFeedback data relates to employee experience rather than candidate evaluation. Organizations access SmartFeedback dashboards and reports to understand retention patterns and employee sentiment, while using SmartHire and other tools to manage the recruiting process.

PRIVACY, ANONYMITY, AND COMPLIANCE
Non-Determinative Role
SmartFeedback is a feedback collection and analysis tool, not a decision-making system. The platform gathers and summarizes employee sentiment, but does not decide outcomes or recommend employment actions. All decisions about how to respond to feedback remain with human managers and HR teams. This non-determinative role is important for both legal compliance and practical effectiveness - retention interventions require human judgment and relationship-building that AI cannot replicate.
Anonymization and Aggregation
Employees can fully anonymize themselves before providing feedback, ensuring they can speak honestly without fear of attribution. When analyzing and reporting data, SmartFeedback does not use personal data in summarization or aggregation. The AI identifies themes and sentiment patterns across responses without requiring or using personally identifiable information beyond what's necessary to route alerts to appropriate managers.
This anonymization capability is critical for capturing candid feedback about sensitive topics. Employees are much more likely to report concerns about management, compensation, or workplace issues when they know responses cannot be traced back to them individually.
Human Interpretation Required
Employers retain full responsibility for evaluating feedback insights and determining any action steps. SmartFeedback provides a confidential channel for employees to engage with an AI agent, and the system records and organizes those conversations, but humans must interpret the meaning and decide how to respond. The AI assists with pattern recognition and theme extraction, but cannot understand the full organizational context that determines appropriate responses.
Data Protection
All feedback data is protected according to data privacy regulations. Organizations can establish escalation protocols for serious concerns flagged by the system, but SmartFeedback itself does not make or enforce employment decisions. The system provides the intelligence; organizations provide the judgment and action.

CADIENT SMARTMATCH™

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Cadient SmartMatch is an AI-powered candidate matching solution built into the Cadient Talent Acquisition platform. It uses advanced semantic analysis and generative AI to automatically score and rank candidates against job requirements, helping recruiters identify the best-fit candidates faster and more accurately than manual screening.
SmartMatch analyzes both candidate resumes and job descriptions to extract skills, experience, and qualifications, then provides an intelligent match score that helps recruiters make confident, data-driven hiring decisions. The system works seamlessly in the background, with all results displayed directly in the Cadient Hiring Management Console (HMC).
KEY CAPABILITIES
Automated Skills Extraction
SmartMatch uses AI to automatically extract skills from candidate resumes and job descriptions. The system recognizes both explicitly stated skills and those implied by work experience and accomplishments. Skills are categorized by type including Technical Skills, Soft Skills, Experience, and Certifications.
Semantic Analysis Matching
Unlike traditional keyword-matching systems, SmartMatch employs semantic analysis to understand the meaning and context of skills and experience. This approach recognizes when candidates describe their abilities differently from how they appear in job descriptions, reducing the risk of overlooking qualified candidates due to terminology differences.
Composite SmartScore
SmartMatch contributes to an overall SmartScore that can include inputs from other Cadient SmartSuite products like SmartTenure and SmartScreen. This composite scoring provides a holistic view of candidate potential and quality across multiple dimensions.
Skills Gap Analysis
For each candidate, SmartMatch identifies specific skills gaps between the candidate's profile and job requirements. This helps recruiters understand exactly where candidates may need additional training or development, enabling more informed hiring decisions and better talent planning.
Job Requirements Intelligence
SmartMatch provides an AI-generated analysis of job descriptions that identifies required skills and categorizes them by type. This allows hiring managers and recruiters to review and refine job descriptions to ensure they accurately represent needed qualifications.
SMARTMATCH IN THE HIRING WORKFLOW
Where SmartMatch Appears
SmartMatch scores are visible throughout the Cadient HMC platform:

Candidate and Applicant Grids: A SmartScore column displays color-coded scores for quick visual assessment. Users can hover over the SmartScore icon to see component scores including the SmartMatch percentage.

Score Interpretation
SmartMatch provides two types of scoring:
Overall SmartScore Alignment:

High Alignment (80-100%): Green indicator - candidate strongly matches job requirements

Moderate Alignment (60-79%): Yellow indicator - candidate has good match with some gaps

Limited Alignment (0-59%): Red indicator - candidate has significant gaps relative to requirements
SmartMatch Skills Match:

Strong Skills Match (80-100%): Green indicator - candidate possesses most required skills

Moderate Skills Match (60-79%): Yellow indicator - candidate has many skills but notable gaps exist

Limited Skills Match (0-59%): Red indicator - substantial skill development would be needed
The system recommends treating applicants within the same score band as equivalent to avoid over-relying on small score differences.

Q: What is Smart Refer (Text2Refer)?
A: Smart Refer (also known as Text2Refer or T2R) is an employee referral automation platform by Cadient Talent that streamlines referral programs through automated campaigns, easy referral submission tools, and comprehensive tracking. It helps companies promote their referral programs and makes it simple for employees to refer candidates.
Q: How does Smart Refer integrate with existing systems?
A: Smart Refer integrates seamlessly with ATS/HRIS systems including Cadient Talent Acquisition (CTA). It supports REST API, file-based integration via SFTP, and can sync employee data, job postings, application status updates, and hire confirmations automatically.
Q: What communication channels does Smart Refer support?
A: Smart Refer supports both email and SMS communication channels for campaigns. It uses the CTX Email Service for HTML emails with tracking and CTX Messaging Service for SMS with link shortening.

8.2 Employee Questions
Q: How do I submit a referral?
A: You can submit referrals in two ways: 1) Through the job portal by browsing open positions and sharing your unique referral link, or 2) Through the chatbot interface which guides you through collecting referral information step by step.
Q: How can I track my referrals?
A: Through the employee portal, you can view all your submitted referrals with real-time status updates. The status workflow shows: Referred, Contacted, Interviewed, Offered, and Hired. You can also see interest levels (Interested, Not Interested, Sent).
Q: When will I receive my referral bonus?

8.3 HR/Recruiter Questions

Q: What payment methods are supported?

Q: Can I export bonus data?
A: Yes, you can export bonus grid results with all columns in CSV/Excel format for payroll processing. Weekly email notifications are also sent for bonuses ready for payment.

CADIENT SMARTSCORE™
Unified Candidate Scoring Across All Evaluation Signals
PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Cadient SmartScore is the unified scoring framework that brings together candidate evaluation insights from multiple SmartSuite products into a single, composite score. Rather than juggling separate scores from resume screening, phone interviews, and other assessment tools, recruiting teams see one comprehensive SmartScore that reflects every signal about a candidate's fit and potential.
SmartScore aggregates data from SmartMatch (resume-job matching) and SmartScreen (AI phone screening) to provide a holistic view of candidate quality. The score updates in real-time as candidates progress through evaluation stages, giving recruiters and hiring managers a constantly evolving picture of how well each candidate aligns with job requirements. This unified approach eliminates the evaluation chaos that occurs when hiring teams must manually compare disconnected data points to make hiring decisions.
THE EVALUATION CHAOS PROBLEM
When candidate evaluation data lives in multiple systems and formats, hiring teams struggle to make confident decisions:

60% of recruiter time is spent comparing inconsistent candidate data from various sources

70% of bad hires result from incomplete interview feedback and fragmented evaluation

4+ different tools are typically used to evaluate a single applicant, creating data silos

Inconsistent decision-making when different hiring team members emphasize different evaluation criteria
The result is spreadsheet debates, gut-feel decisions, and hiring managers asking "But what do you really think about this candidate?" because the data doesn't tell a clear story. Teams need a way to synthesize all evaluation signals into one trustworthy measure of candidate quality.
HOW SMARTSCORE WORKS
Component Products
SmartScore combines evaluation data from these Cadient SmartSuite products:

SmartMatch: AI-powered resume and job description comparison that scores how well candidate skills and experience align with job requirements

SmartScreen: AI-powered structured phone screening that evaluates communication skills, confidence, experience relevance, and job fit through conversational interviews
Organizations must have both SmartMatch and SmartScreen to use SmartScore. The framework requires multiple evaluation signals to create a meaningful composite score. SmartScore is included when organizations purchase both component products - there is no separate fee for the unified scoring framework.
Composite Scoring
SmartScore uses a weighted algorithm to combine scores from SmartMatch and SmartScreen into a single 0-100 composite score. Organizations can customize the relative importance of each component to reflect what matters most for different roles. For example, a technical position might weight SmartMatch skills alignment more heavily, while a customer-facing role might emphasize SmartScreen communication and confidence scores.
The specific weighting formulas are proprietary, but organizations have control over the general emphasis given to each component. If an organization doesn't use one of the component products, that signal simply doesn't factor into the SmartScore calculation.
Real-Time Updates
SmartScore updates instantly as candidates complete each evaluation stage. When a candidate applies and SmartMatch scores their resume, an initial SmartScore appears based solely on that first signal. When the candidate completes a SmartScreen phone interview, the SmartScore immediately updates to reflect the new information. This real-time updating means recruiters always see the most current, comprehensive view of candidate quality.
There is no delay or batch processing - scores reflect all available data the moment each component completes its evaluation. However, the system does not maintain score history, so users see only the current SmartScore rather than how it has changed over time.
Score Interpretation
SmartScore uses color-coded bands to provide quick visual interpretation:

High Alignment (80-100): Green indicator - candidate demonstrates strong fit across multiple evaluation dimensions

Moderate Alignment (60-79): Yellow indicator - candidate shows good potential with some gaps or areas for development

Limited Alignment (0-59): Red indicator - candidate has significant gaps relative to job requirements
These visual indicators appear alongside the numeric score in candidate grids and profiles, allowing recruiters to quickly identify top candidates at a glance. The system also flags candidates who meet certain score criteria, helping recruiters focus their attention on the most promising applicants.
WHERE SMARTSCORE APPEARS
SmartScore is visible throughout the Cadient Hiring Management Console (HMC):

Candidate Grids: SmartScore column displays the composite score with color coding for quick scanning

Applicant Grids: See SmartScore for all applicants to a specific position

Candidate Profiles: SmartScore prominently displayed on individual candidate detail pages

Application Detail Records (ADR): SmartScore visible on the overview tab alongside other candidate information

Hiring Workflows: Recruiters can sort, filter, and prioritize candidates by SmartScore
All user roles with appropriate permissions can see SmartScore. The system does not currently provide drill-down capability to see individual component scores that make up the composite SmartScore - users see the unified score rather than the underlying breakdown.
BUSINESS VALUE
Faster, More Confident Decisions
SmartScore eliminates the time recruiters spend manually comparing inconsistent data from different evaluation tools. Instead of pulling up resume scores in one system, phone screening notes in another, and trying to synthesize everything into a recommendation, recruiters see one clear signal that already incorporates all available information. This acceleration doesn't cut corners - it simply removes the friction from decision-making.
Alignment Across Hiring Teams
When recruiters, hiring managers, and HR teams all work from the same score, conversations become more productive. Rather than debating which evaluation criteria matter most or trying to compare gut feelings, teams can focus on whether candidates above a certain SmartScore threshold meet their needs. This shared framework creates consistency in how candidates are evaluated across the organization.

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Cadient SmartScreen is an AI-powered automated phone interview system that conducts initial candidate screenings using natural language processing and conversational AI. The system conducts free-flowing, natural phone conversations with candidates, asking role-specific questions, analyzing responses in real-time, and providing structured scoring to help recruiters identify the most promising candidates quickly.
Unlike traditional screening methods that require recruiters to spend hours on the phone, SmartScreen handles the entire initial interview process autonomously. Each candidate applying for the same role receives an identical structured interview, ensuring consistency and fairness while dramatically reducing the time recruiters spend on initial screenings. The AI analyzes candidate responses against standardized criteria and provides comprehensive scoring, transcripts, and insights for recruiter review.

COMPLIANCE AND BEST PRACTICES
TCPA Compliance
Organizations using SmartTexting must ensure compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), which regulates text messaging to consumers. Candidates should have clear ability to opt out of text communications, and organizations should maintain records of consent where applicable. SmartTexting includes opt-out functionality to support TCPA compliance.
Professional Communication Standards
While texting is inherently more casual than email, recruiters should maintain professional standards in their messages. Messages should be clear, respectful, and appropriate for business communication. The informality of texting can actually improve candidate rapport, but organizations should establish guidelines for tone and content to ensure consistency.
Data Protection and Privacy
All text message content and candidate phone numbers are protected according to data privacy regulations. The complete message history stored in SmartHire is subject to the same data protection policies as other candidate information. Organizations control who has access to view text conversations and can establish retention policies aligned with their legal requirements.
Response Time Expectations
Because candidates respond to texts so quickly (90 seconds on average), they also expect relatively quick responses from recruiters. Organizations should establish internal standards for text response times and ensure adequate recruiter capacity to maintain conversations. The immediacy that makes texting effective also creates an expectation of engagement that organizations need to meet.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is SmartTexting?
SmartTexting is Cadient's two-way SMS messaging system built into the SmartHire ATS. It enables recruiters to communicate with candidates via text message, maintaining complete message history in the ATS while providing the speed and immediacy that candidates prefer.
Is SmartTexting two-way or just outbound messaging?
SmartTexting is fully two-way. Recruiters can send texts to candidates, and candidates can reply freely. Those replies appear immediately in the ATS for recruiters to see and respond to, creating natural back-and-forth conversations.
Do recruiters use their personal phones or does Cadient provide numbers?
Cadient provides dedicated phone numbers for SmartTexting. Recruiters send and receive texts through the SmartHire interface rather than using personal phones. Organizations can have dedicated numbers for different locations or teams.
Can texts be automated or is everything manual?
SmartTexting supports both manual and scheduled automated messages. Recruiters can type and send texts immediately or schedule messages to be sent automatically at specific times, such as interview reminders or follow-up prompts.
What do recruiters typically use SmartTexting for?
Common uses include interest checks, interview scheduling, interview confirmations and reminders, answering candidate questions, providing shift updates, requesting documents, sending status updates, and coordinating quick logistics. Basically any communication that needs to happen quickly between recruiters and candidates.
Can we include links in text messages?
Yes. Messages can include links for interview calendar invitations, application forms, location details, or other relevant resources within the standard constraints of SMS messaging.
Does SmartTexting work with external ATS systems?
No. SmartTexting only works with Cadient SmartHire ATS. The deep integration with SmartHire is what enables the seamless experience and complete message history tracking.
Where do recruiters see text conversations?
Text conversations appear both in individual candidate profiles within SmartHire and in a dedicated messaging tab. The candidate profile view shows that specific candidate's history, while the messaging tab provides an inbox-style interface for managing multiple conversations.
Can multiple recruiters see the same candidate's text thread?
Yes. All hiring team members with appropriate permissions can view the complete text conversation history for any candidate. This prevents duplicate outreach and ensures continuity when different team members interact with the same candidate.
Are there analytics on text engagement?
Yes. SmartTexting tracks response rates, message volume, and engagement metrics. Organizations can understand which types of messages generate the highest candidate response and use this data to improve communication effectiveness.
What's the difference between SmartTexting and SmartSource SMS capability?
SmartSource is for getting candidates into the pipeline through automated outreach when they apply. SmartTexting is for engaging candidates already in the pipeline through two-way conversations. SmartSource automates systematic communications, while SmartTexting enables personal dialogue and coordination.
How do you ensure TCPA compliance?
SmartTexting includes opt-out functionality to support TCPA compliance. Organizations should ensure they have appropriate consent for texting candidates and maintain clear opt-out procedures. The Cadient team provides guidance on compliance requirements during implementation.
Can candidates opt out of text communications?
Yes. SmartTexting supports candidate opt-out consistent with TCPA requirements. Organizations establish clear procedures for handling opt-out requests and maintaining appropriate communication boundaries.
What's the response time for candidates receiving texts?
Based on Cadient data, the average response time to a text message is 90 seconds. This is dramatically faster than email response times which can be hours or days if candidates respond at all.
Does SmartTexting reduce interview no-shows?
Yes. Organizations using SmartTexting for timely interview reminders see approximately 50% reduction in no-shows compared to email-only communication. The immediacy of text reminders ensures candidates actually see the message and can confirm or reschedule if needed.
What industries is SmartTexting designed for?
SmartTexting is designed for the same industries as other Cadient SmartSuite products: retail, healthcare, hospitality, restaurants, and distribution. These industries hire hourly workers who overwhelmingly prefer text communication, making SMS the natural channel for recruiting engagement.
Can we customize message templates?
Yes. Organizations can create custom message templates for common communications and scheduled automated messages. Templates can be adapted for different roles, locations, or communication scenarios.
How quickly can SmartTexting be implemented?

What training is provided?
SmartTexting includes custom onboarding covering how to send texts, manage conversations, schedule automated messages, use message history features, and maintain compliance. The goal is ensuring recruiters can effectively use SMS to improve candidate engagement.
Does SmartTexting work for all hiring volumes?
SmartTexting works for any hiring volume but delivers the most value in high-volume operations where the speed and efficiency of text communication becomes essential for managing many active candidates simultaneously.

Partner Integration Guide: WOTC Check (Standard) & Vendor Support
Annual Investment: $3,500/year

Product Overview
The WOTC Check (Standard) is a robust, automated integration framework designed to identify federal tax credit eligibility during the hiring lifecycle. By connecting your Applicant Tracking System (ATS) with a Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) provider, businesses can capture significant tax savings—up to $9,600 per eligible hire—without manual administrative overhead.

While our baseline integration is optimized for ADP WOTC, our architecture is built to be vendor-agnostic, allowing us to support a wide range of third-party tax credit processors.


How We Support Third-Party & New Vendors
Our system is designed with a "pluggable" architecture. This means we don't just offer a static connection; we provide a flexible framework that can be adapted to your preferred vendor’s specifications.

1. Universal XML & HTTPS Standards
Protocol Compatibility: We utilize industry-standard HTTPS and XML file formats for data exchange. This ensures that any modern third-party vendor can receive and process our data packets without proprietary hardware or software.
Real-Time Frequency: Our system is capable of Real-time triggers, meaning we can push data to a new vendor the second an action occurs in your workflow.
2. Configurable Integration Libraries
Agnostic Logic: We store integration logic in dedicated libraries (e.g., BiLoLLC_Integrations or IntegrationADPWOTC).
Custom Field Mapping: We use "System Variables" like WOTCProvider to tell the system which vendor to talk to. For new vendors, we simply update the provider variable and point the stylesheet to the new endpoint.
3. Location-Based Routing
Our system supports complex organizational structures where different branches might use different vendors or participation levels.

Custom Security Logic: We use fields like adpSecurityCode and adpURLSuffix to ensure data is routed to the correct vendor account.
Participation Checks: If a specific location is not yet ready for a new vendor, our logic automatically bypasses the request to prevent errors, as seen in our Yum! Brands implementation.

Example Implementation: The Yum! Brands Model
We successfully manage high-volume integrations for Taco Bell, Pizza Hut, and KFC. This model serves as the blueprint for how we onboard new vendor support:
Trigger Points: The system initiates a check at the completion of the Job Application Form (JAF) and again during the hiring workflow.+2
Hiring Workflow Nodes: We can map vendor calls to specific "nodes" such as "Hired" or "Arrived at Work" depending on the vendor's requirement for a "Hire Check" vs. an "Initial Check".
Dynamic Styling: We utilize .xsl stylesheets (like AdpHireXml.xsl) which can be customized to transform our data into the specific format required by a new third-party vendor.





Resilience and Maintenance
When moving to a new third-party vendor, data integrity is our priority.
Automated Retry Logic: Our "Retry Failed Requests" trigger runs every 1,200 seconds. If a third-party vendor's server is briefly down, our system will automatically attempt to resend the applicant data until the credit is captured.
Comprehensive Logging: We provide [INFORM] and [ERROR] logs that track every interaction with the vendor, allowing for rapid troubleshooting during the transition to a new provider.

Product Overview: WOTC Check (Standard)
Annual Investment: $3,500/year

What is WOTC Check?
The Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) Check is a high-performance integration solution designed to help employers identify and claim federal tax credits for hiring individuals from specific target groups. These groups often include veterans, recipients of various food and family assistance programs, and residents of designated empowerment zones.

By automating the screening process within the digital application and hiring workflow, the WOTC Check removes the administrative burden of manual paperwork. It transforms a complex legislative incentive into a background utility that consistently drives bottom-line savings by reducing federal income tax liability with every qualifying hire.


Key Uses & Business Benefits
Instant Eligibility Screening: An initial check is triggered automatically upon the completion of the Job Application Form (JAF), categorizing applicants as "Ineligible" or "Potentially Eligible" immediately.
Integrated Hiring Workflow: The system seamlessly requests form links and tax documentation during the hiring process, such as when a candidate is moved to "Hired" or "Arrived at Work" status.
Real-Time Data Exchange: Data is transmitted in real-time via secure HTTPS using XML file formats, ensuring no delays in the hiring process.
Automated Data Correction: A built-in "Retry Failed ADPWOTC Requests" trigger runs every 1,200 seconds to automatically process applicants who may have initially failed due to minor data entry errors like invalid Zip Codes or Dates of Birth.
Detailed Audit Trail: The system captures and displays "Tax Credit Confirmation Codes" (payroll codes) directly on the applicant's overview tab, providing a clear record for accounting and tax filing.

Our Example Usage: Yum! Brands Integration
The effectiveness of this product is best demonstrated by its successful deployment for Yum! Brands, covering global entities including Taco Bell, Pizza Hut, and KFC.
1. Universal Seeker Site Configuration
Yum! Brands utilizes three primary seeker sites—KFC, Pizza Hut, and Taco Bell—all configured with the same robust ADP WOTC integration. This ensures a unified experience for both applicants and recruiters across the entire corporate umbrella.
2. Multi-Stage Validation
The integration occurs in two critical phases to ensure maximum credit capture:
The Application Phase: Using the wotcIntegration.xml workflow, the system performs a "First WOTC Request" as the candidate applies. This gives hiring managers visibility into the tax value of a candidate before the interview even begins.
The Hiring Phase: Once a manager decides to hire, a specialized "Hire and Forms" request is triggered. For example, in the Pizza Hut workflow, this occurs at the "Arrived at Work" node for specific positions like Delivery Drivers, Servers, and Shift Managers.
3. Location-Specific Customization
To manage a massive footprint of stores, the system uses custom fields—adpSecurityCode and adpURLSuffix—to verify if a specific location is participating in the integration. If a location is not participating, the system is smart enough to bypass the request, preventing unnecessary data transmission.
