# Cadient Product Documentation - Feature-Wise Summary

This document provides a detailed summary of Cadient's product features, their functionality, and the specific problems they solve. This documentation is intended for use as context in Cadient's AI chatbot system and for training vector databases.

---

## 1. Core AI Suite & Intelligence (SmartSuite™)

### **Decision Point™**
*   **Feature:** The advanced AI logic engine that powers Cadient's predictive capabilities.
*   **What it is:** A proprietary decision-support framework that uses machine learning to analyze candidate data against historical hiring and performance outcomes.
*   **Problem it solves:** Eliminates hiring based on "gut feel" or inconsistent criteria. It provides objective, data-driven recommendations that standardize evaluation across the entire organization.

### **SmartScore™**
*   **Feature:** Unified Candidate Scoring.
*   **What it is:** A composite 0-100 score that aggregates evaluation signals from multiple sources, including SmartMatch (resume fit) and SmartScreen (AI phone screening). It uses color-coded bands (Green/Yellow/Red) for quick interpretation.
*   **Problem it solves:** Solves "Evaluation Chaos." Recruiters no longer need to manually compare disconnected data points from resumes, phone screens, and interviews; SmartScore provides one clear, holistic signal of candidate quality.

### **SmartTenure™**
*   **Feature:** Predictive Retention Scoring.
*   **What it is:** An AI tool that predicts the likelihood of a candidate staying with the company long-term. It analyzes career stability patterns, "tenure decay curves," and narrative coherence in work history, benchmarking against industry norms.
*   **Problem it solves:** Addresses the high cost of early-stage turnover (often 42% in hourly roles). It helps organizations "hire once, not twice" by identifying candidates with high "stay-power" before an offer is made.

## 2. Candidate Sourcing & Referrals

### **SmartSource™ (Recruit)**
*   **Feature:** AI-Powered Candidate Discovery.
*   **What it is:** Uses AI to scan top job boards and the internal "Silver Medalist" database to identify and recommend high-fit candidates for open roles.
*   **Problem it solves:** Reduces reliance on "post-and-pray" recruiting. It proactively finds qualified talent even when applicant volume is low, ensuring pipelines remain full.

### **SmartRefer / Text2Refer™**
*   **Feature:** Mobile-First Employee Referral Management.
*   **What it is:** Software that enables current employees to refer candidates via unique tracking links or SMS. It automates campaign tracking, bonus calculations, and status updates for referrers.
*   **Problem it solves:** Traditional referral programs are often manual and hard to track. SmartRefer mobilizes the workforce as a sourcing channel, capitalizing on the fact that referred hires typically have 47% higher retention.

---

## 3. Communication & Engagement

### **SmartSource™ (Outreach)**
*   **Feature:** Automated Candidate Communication.
*   **What it is:** Automatically sends personalized, timely emails and texts based on triggers (e.g., application received, status change).
*   **Problem it solves:** Eliminates the "Recruiter Inbox Black Hole." It ensures 100% of candidates receive immediate engagement, reducing ghosting (which is 5x more likely when outreach is delayed).

### **SmartTexting™**
*   **Feature:** Two-Way Branded SMS Communication.
*   **What it is:** Enables recruiters to text candidates directly from the Cadient Hiring Management Console (HMC). It supports manual chats, automated interview reminders, and "Text Apply" (applying via SMS).
*   **Problem it solves:** Solves low engagement with email. 73% of hourly applicants prefer text. SmartTexting delivers a 90-second average response time and reduces interview no-shows by up to 50%.

### **Text Campaigns**
*   **Feature:** Ad-hoc Bulk SMS Outreach.
*   **What it is:** Tools for sending targeted SMS blasts to segmented candidate audiences for hiring events, seasonal surges, or re-engagement.
*   **Problem it solves:** Efficiently reaches thousands of potential applicants simultaneously for high-volume needs without the manual effort of 1-to-1 outreach.

---

## 4. Evaluation & Screening

### **SmartMatch™**
*   **Feature:** Intelligent Resume/Job Matching.
*   **What it is:** A semantic search engine that scores candidates based on how well their skills and experience align with job descriptions, going beyond simple keyword matching.
*   **Problem it solves:** Manually reviewing hundreds of resumes is time-consuming. SmartMatch automatically surfaces the most qualified applicants, allowing recruiters to focus on the top 10% immediately.

### **SmartScreen™**
*   **Feature:** AI-Powered Phone Screening.
*   **What it is:** An automated system that conducts natural-language phone interviews using conversational AI. It evaluates communication, confidence, and experience, providing transcripts and scores.
*   **Problem it solves:** Phone screening is the #1 bottleneck in high-volume hiring. SmartScreen handles the volume autonomously 24/7, ensuring every candidate is screened consistently and fairly without manual recruiter effort.

### **SmartInterview™**
*   **Feature:** Structured In-person Interviewing.
*   **What it is:** Provides hiring managers with structured interview guides and scoring rubrics directly within the HMC to evaluate soft skills and cultural fit.
*   **Problem it solves:** Standardizes the final interview stage, reducing bias and ensuring all candidates for the same role are evaluated against the same criteria.

### **SmartFeedback™**
*   **Feature:** AI-Driven Post-Hire Feedback.
*   **What it is:** Uses AI voice calls to gather feedback from new hires and managers shortly after onboarding.
*   **Problem it solves:** Identifies early-tenure warning signs to prevent "quick quits." It provides leadership with insights into why people leave or stay in their first 90 days.

## 5. Video Solutions

### **Video Add-Ons & Enhancements**
*   **Feature:** Realistic Job Preview (RJP) & Confirmation Videos.
*   **What it is:** Incorporates authentic employee videos into job postings and adds mandatory video acknowledgment steps in the application.
*   **Problem it solves:** Transparency. It gives candidates a real look at the job "warts and all," which results in a 25-40% reduction in early turnover because candidates know what to expect before they start.

### **Video Interviewing (Powered by SparkHire)**
*   **Feature:** Asynchronous and Live Video Interviews.
*   **What it is:** Integrated one-way video screens where candidates record responses on their schedule, and live panel interview tools.
*   **Problem it solves:** Scheduling conflicts and geographic barriers. It reduces time-to-first-interview by 50% and allows teams to collaborate on candidate reviews asynchronously.

---

## 6. Compliance, Analytics & Logistics

### **WOTC Check (Standard & New Vendor)**
*   **Feature:** Automated Federal Tax Credit Screening.
*   **What it is:** Integrated screening for federal tax credits (up to $9,600 per hire) for specific target groups. It is vendor-agnostic but optimized for ADP.
*   **Problem it solves:** Captures significant tax savings without manual administrative work. It automates the complex legislative process and data transmission to tax processors.

### **Drug Test (Standard)**
*   **Feature:** Integrated Digital Drug Screening.
*   **What it is:** Automates the drug testing process from conditional offer to result. It provides digital Chain of Custody (eCCF) and automated facility directions.
*   **Problem it solves:** Replaces manual paper tracking. It ensures compliance with state-specific laws and automatically flags "No-Shows" to keep the hiring pipeline moving.

### **Talent Acquisition Dashboard (TAD)**
*   **Feature:** End-to-End Recruiting Analytics.
*   **What it is:** A centralized reporting hub (powered by AWS QuickSight) that visualizes funnel performance, sourcing effectiveness, and retention metrics.
*   **Problem it solves:** Provides a "single source of truth" for recruiting data. Leadership can identify bottlenecks in real-time and optimize the hiring process based on actionable insights rather than assumptions.

---

## 7. Support & Integration

### **SmartHire™ (ATS)**
*   **Feature:** Core Applicant Tracking System.
*   **What it is:** The central platform that manages the entire high-volume, hourly recruiting workflow from job posting to offer.
*   **Problem it solves:** Built specifically for the complexities of distributed, high-volume hiring (like retail and hospitality) where speed and scale are more critical than in traditional corporate ATS models.
